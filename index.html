Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

PhoneBreaker ("the App") is developed by Lucas as a personal project. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. Data Collection

PhoneBreaker does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The App does not require an account, login, or any personal information to function.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Spin session history (number of rotations, duration, RPM, date)
  • Your all-time best score
  • App preferences (session delay setting)

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer or any third party. It can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the App.

3. Sensors

The App uses your device's gyroscope and accelerometer to measure spin sessions. This sensor data is processed in real time on your device and is never stored beyond the current session or transmitted anywhere.

4. Third-Party Services

PhoneBreaker does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any data from any user, including children. It is safe for all ages.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a future version of the App, the updated policy will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer at: lucas@example.com
